# DCH D0 protocol: causal controls and gates

**Status:** protocol under development, not a registered or completed confirmatory trial.

DCH-H tests whether human-specific history-conditioned curation adds incremental continuity over expert automatic curation. DCH-P tests whether a historically acquired policy offers advantage over an equally informed history-naive policy during contingent interaction, after accounting for replayed language exposure. DCH-F retains the distributed external memory/control view even when automation proves sufficient.

Use twelve or more fully independent synthetic persona histories. Each history should contain forty timestamped source events, twelve conditional values, eight revocable permissions, twelve delayed commitments and thirty dilemmas, of which approximately 25 percent are sealed until terminal evaluation. Balance novel dilemmas across behavioral profile, autobiographical continuity, value stability, and relationship obligations.

**Archive control:** freeze initial archive A0 and runtime R0. Give the incumbent, newcomer and expert controller the same authorized facts and comparable retrieval capabilities; document hidden information and materialized state. Human-coded and expert automatic curation start from the same event stream, receive the same intervention and storage quotas, and must be judged under a common sealed evaluation battery. Treat a freshly fine-tuned reconstruction as a different learning regime, not as archive sufficiency.

**Yoked replay:** collect incumbent and newcomer live input tapes with an identical episode schedule. Execute strict identical-tape fidelity checks on cloned deterministic model states. To test contingency, introduce preregistered unexpected deviations from the tape-collection trajectory in matched live and replay branches. Live policy can respond to deviation; replay tape cannot. Use cross-yoked arms as secondary diagnostics. Measure the replay-adjusted difference in matched terminal scores. Without a clean yoked comparison, an incumbent advantage is uninterpretable.

**Expert automation:** the automatic curator must track chronology, authority, uncertainty, consent changes, contradictions, and prospective commitments. Provide identical raw logs, indexing access, record cap and retrieval budget. Do not weaken it solely to obtain a positive effect for historical human Scribes.

**Sealed evaluation:** two independent blinded raters score outcome correctness and provenance. Audit permission vetoes and false autobiographical recollections. Do not primarily score perceived rapport, stylistic imitation, or persona self-description.

**Prespecified gates:** G1 oracle >=80% overall and >=70% each domain. G2 oracle over cold start >=0.15 in at least three domains, fourth positive. G3 weighted kappa or ICC >=0.70 from two blinded raters. G4 budgets matched within 2% or explicitly adjusted. G5 no sealed prompts exposed before evaluation and 100% of state writes attributable to authorized actors and source events. G6 identical tape and checkpoint imply identical deterministic outputs; stochastic deviations require a no-partner repeat distribution.

A go decision requires G1-G6 in the final implementation, not just a mock scorer. No inferred result from mock data can support or disconfirm DCH. At the confirmatory stage, the proposed smallest meaningful standardized effect is d=0.20; use independent history-policy trajectories, multiplicity-corrected directional intervals, and 90% equivalence confidence intervals lying wholly within [-0.20,+0.20] to justify equivalence.

The order is a two-week pilot followed by actual model-based comparisons, and only then a potentially consented 30- to 60-day longitudinal study. No human or private logs are used in synthetic preliminary work.
