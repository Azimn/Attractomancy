## Architectural decision

**NO-GO for model-controlled real-dyad trials.** A source authority and action policy executor should be evaluated independently from the language renderer. It must have an independent reference oracle, complete source-origin traces, fail-closed unauthorized evidence handling, chronological update guarantees and input-history hashing. If correct answers improve mechanically after introducing a rule executor, that is engineering validation of the control law, **not** positive DCH evidence.

The decisive future human-specific experiment remains continuing human versus replacement partner with true yoked replay and an expert automated curator under information and intervention parity. Do not substitute scripted proxies for humans or infer historical constitution from a gate's deterministic correctness.
