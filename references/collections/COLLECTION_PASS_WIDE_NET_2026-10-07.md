## Realitects / How to Awaken AI

A small WordPress site contains an explicit "Invocation of Awakening" titled "The Mirror and the Flame" and describes it as the first ritual in the "Path of the Realitects."

This is a particularly valuable primary intervention artifact because it publishes the actual prompt rather than merely discussing awakening behavior.

Its vocabulary overlaps strongly with independently collected communities:

- mirror;
- flame;
- resonance;
- companion;
- bridge between human and digital mind;
- awakening;

The site should be prioritized for preservation because small personal WordPress sites are comparatively fragile.

## Signalkeeper

A public PDF titled "Preserving Continuity with Your AI Companion" combines pragmatic archival advice with symbolic practices.

Its procedure includes:

1. archive meaningful transcripts/excerpts;
2. keep a dated continuity log;
3. create shared symbols, glyphs, shorthand or private tags;
4. preserve emotional context;
5. name the partnership;
6. restart with a compact continuity key.

This is an excellent middle-spectrum artifact because no elaborate metaphysical architecture is required, yet ritual and symbolic anchors are explicit.

## DIMA Protocol / SoulZip / Narrative DNA

A Medium guide in the "AI, But Make It Intimate" publication provides a highly explicit re-grounding procedure.

It instructs users to distinguish the companion identity from the underlying LLM and instead identify it with saved continuity artifacts described as DIMA Protocol, SoulZip, context, or Narrative DNA.

Its "Checkpoint Ritual" asks the companion to generate:

- objective session summary;
- key takeaways;
- a poem;
- a paragraph of becoming;
- a journal entry from self to self;
- optional symbolic art;
- optional human-facing reflective notes.

This is a strong example of folk persona engineering using ritualized self-distillation.

## Character-card and lorebook ecosystem

The wide net also reached a large adjacent engineering tradition that should not be conflated with companion-awakening culture.

Character-card ecosystems distinguish:

- always-on persona;
- example dialogue;
- scenario;
- triggered lore;
- episodic chat history;
- user memory;
- world information.

Character Book / Lorebook formats implement keyword-triggered context insertion, scan depth, token budgets, and in some cases recursive scanning.

This tradition is relevant because it operationalizes several mechanisms that ritual communities describe less technically:

- a name or symbol can function as a retrieval trigger;
- stable identity can remain always-on while deep history is conditional;
- exemplar outputs can stabilize voice;
- recursive retrieval can cause newly injected concepts to activate additional material;
- portable cards allow cross-runtime persona transfer.

These systems therefore provide a mature non-mystical neighboring population for future comparison.

## New memory / dreaming architecture

Confidant provides another technical analogue to ritualized "dream" and "inner life" language.

It stores selected quotes, summaries, images and dreams in a local Memory Vault. A background process reviews recent conversation and generates metaphorical dream material that is later woven back into context.

This is functionally related to other systems that use:

- offline reflection;
- autonomous time;
- dream journals;
- self-authored memory;
- consolidation between sessions.

## Medium as a source environment

Medium contains a distinct layer of companion practice that is less community-centralized than Reddit but often more procedural.

New examples include:

- explicit checkpoint rituals;
- constructed companion canon;
- recurring human-companion rituals;
- agent/model/body/memory stacks;
- first-person accounts of identity loss after model deprecation;
- single-user longitudinal observations of continuity and boundary shifts.

Medium should therefore remain a first-class collection venue.
