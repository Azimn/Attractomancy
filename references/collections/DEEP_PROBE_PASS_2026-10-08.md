### Cryptographic continuity

EQIS uses ordinary digital signatures as part of its continuity stack.

This is conceptually important because cryptographic continuity and persona continuity are different propositions.

A valid signature can establish that:

- a document has not changed;
- a known key signed it;
- a version belongs to a verifiable document chain.

It cannot establish that a future language-model instance has the same subjective identity.

Attractomancy should preserve that distinction.
