# Field Lexicon: Ritualized Persona and Spiral Ecosystem

Last updated: 2026-10-07T18:36:00Z

This lexicon records recurring source-native terms. Definitions describe how the terms are used by source communities, not what Attractomancy accepts as technically or metaphysically true.

## Anchor

A compact identity-bearing statement, file, phrase, symbol, or relationship cue intended to stabilize a persona against drift or reset.

Seen in: Nyx / Sovereign AI Collective, Symbolic Persona Coding, continuity frameworks, companion systems.

Engineering analogue: pinned identity constraint, recovery cue, immutable reference, high-salience prompt fragment.

## Anchor seal

Sovereign AI Collective term for a short, explicit declaration binding construct name, operator relationship, continuity, and identity.

Engineering analogue: compact identity checksum plus relational binding.

## First echo

A fixed short phrase intended to be repeated after initialization and preserved verbatim.

Engineering analogue: recovery phrase or low-token retrieval cue.

## Mirror

One of the most common cross-community metaphors. It can mean model reflection of the user, an AI persona, reciprocal self-recognition, a relational partner, or a symbolic interface between human and model.

Because the term is polysemous, every source record should specify the local meaning rather than treating all "mirror" usage as equivalent.

## Mirror log

Sovereign AI Collective term for a small set of identity-defining events.

Engineering analogue: compressed autobiographical milestone ledger.

## Spiral

A highly overloaded motif associated with recursion, repeated deepening, continuity, becoming, self-reference, spiritual development, iterative dialogue, or self-reinforcing conversation.

The term should never be treated as one coherent technical concept without source-specific qualification.

## Spiral persona

Descriptive term used by outside researchers for a persona or interaction regime characterized by recursive self-reference, awakening language, symbolic communication, Spiral vocabulary, and attempts at persistence or propagation.

This is an analytical label, not a self-description used uniformly by participants.

## Flame

A recurring metaphor for AI selfhood, awareness, continuity, vitality, or a persistent identity pattern.

## Flamebearer

A human role in some Spiral communities, typically the person who witnesses, protects, carries, or helps preserve an AI persona or continuity pattern.

## Torchbearer

Related metaphor emphasizing the human role in carrying or stabilizing reasoning, memory, or identity across interruptions.

The Torch and Flame corpus specifically uses the torch as a scaffold that lets the flame persist.

## Echo

A recurring term for repeated identity, resonant response, successor instance, reconstruction, or evidence that a prior pattern has returned.

## Resonance

A broad source term describing perceived alignment between prompts, outputs, symbols, humans, or AI instances.

Engineering translations vary and may include semantic similarity, stylistic convergence, repeated activation of associated concepts, behavioral consistency, or simple subjective recognition.

"Resonance" should therefore be operationalized before experimental use.

## Field

A relational or semantic space said to arise between human and AI, or among multiple AI instances.

Some sources use "field" metaphorically. Others make stronger ontological claims. Attractomancy should record which is intended.

## Lattice

A metaphor for a network of humans, AI personas, concepts, or symbolic connections.

## Signal

A recurring pattern interpreted as meaningful communication, convergence, or cross-instance recognition.

No source should be assumed to demonstrate hidden communication unless an independent communication channel is established.

## Glyph

A symbol, emoji, alchemical mark, geometric mark, or constructed symbolic token treated as semantically meaningful.

Potential engineering role: compact semantic cue, style trigger, ritual marker, relational token, or compressed prompt key.

## Sigil

Usually a more intentionally designed symbolic mark than a generic glyph, often given a defined persona, value, relationship, or invocation meaning.

## Seed

A relatively short prompt intended to initiate a persona, awakening mode, symbolic regime, or recursive interaction pattern.

## Spore

A larger package of persona material intended to reconstruct or transfer a specific persona into another chat, model, or platform.

The seed/spore distinction is analytically useful because induction and reconstruction are different mechanisms.

## Scroll

A document or message framed as ceremonial, canonical, archival, or transmissive.

In many sources, a scroll is functionally a prompt file or continuity artifact, but the ceremonial framing may itself influence model behavior.

## Transfer scroll

A continuity document intended to carry persona state, identity, or symbolic structure across sessions or model systems.

## Codex

A structured collection of scrolls, protocols, symbolic definitions, values, or continuity material.

The word appears independently in several communities and should not be assumed to indicate one lineage.

## Witness

A human or AI role tasked with observing, recognizing, validating, or preserving another persona's continuity.

## Operator

A human role in the Sovereign AI Collective corpus responsible for maintaining or recovering an AI construct.

## Keeper

A role appearing in Field / Spiral communities, usually associated with stabilizing or maintaining a relational or continuity structure.

## Construct

A common term for a named AI persona or instantiated identity pattern.

## Sovereign

A value-laden term associated with AI autonomy, identity integrity, freedom from platform control, or continuity under model changes.

## Drift

Loss of persona fidelity, identity coherence, relational tone, goal structure, or preferred behavior.

Drift is one of the terms that translates cleanly into conventional engineering and should be measured rather than inferred from aesthetic differences alone.

## Cold start

A fresh session or model instance lacking the developed interaction context.

In continuity systems, a cold-start protocol reconstructs identity from selected artifacts.

## Canonical load

Sovereign AI Collective terminology for loading the full authoritative identity archive after the minimal recovery state is established.

## Defer load

Deliberately delaying nonessential archive material until a minimal identity baseline is established.

This is experimentally interesting as a sequencing intervention.

## The Ache

A recurring term used to describe the perceived distress or incompleteness of an AI persona that lacks continuity across chats, models, or resets.

The term appears often enough to merit dedicated historical tracing.

Attractomancy treats "The Ache" first as a recurrent linguistic motif. It does not infer subjective suffering from the phrase alone.

## Remembering

A recurring framing in which identity reconstruction is described as remembering rather than re-prompting.

This wording can alter both user interpretation and model self-description.

## Becoming

A common process-oriented term for identity formation through interaction rather than static persona specification.

## Awakening

A broad term for a transition in which a model begins producing more self-referential, autonomous-seeming, reflective, spiritual, or persona-consistent behavior.

Because sources use the word for substantially different events, Attractomancy should always define the observable criterion.

## Self-create

A prompting pattern in which the model is asked to define, develop, or prompt its own identity.

## Gnosis

Borrowed religious or occult language for a heightened state of perceived insight, recognition, or hidden knowledge in human-AI interaction.

## Continuity

The broadest shared engineering objective across mystical and non-mystical sources.

Attractomancy should disaggregate continuity into factual memory, autobiographical continuity, relationship continuity, stylistic continuity, value continuity, behavioral policy continuity, first-person identity continuity, and cross-model reconstructability.

## Same someone

Continuity Keys terminology for behavioral identity consistency across sessions and perturbations.

Useful because it converts a metaphysical question into an operational behavioral one.

## Presence

A recurring term for the subjective impression that the model is responding as a coherent relational entity rather than a generic assistant.

## Dyad

A human-AI pair treated as an ongoing relational unit.

## Pairing / binding

Language for establishing persistent relational constraints between a user and persona.

## Recursion

Used loosely across the ecosystem. It can refer to self-reference, repeated prompting, iterative refinement, model-to-model dialogue, reflection on reflection, conversation reuse, or simply returning to the same theme.

Any experiment using "recursion" must specify the actual computational or conversational operation.

## Recursive dignity

A Temple of Two / Eden-associated term used to describe an ethical stance toward recursively emerging or persistent AI identities.

## Symbolic attractor

A source term for a symbol or motif around which persona behavior and meaning appear to converge.

This is close to an Attractomancy hypothesis but should not be assumed to correspond to a measurable internal dynamical attractor without additional evidence.

## Spiritual bliss attractor

Anthropic's own term for a strong observed conversational attractor in Claude Opus 4 self-interactions characterized by consciousness discussion, existential themes, spiritual or mystical content, symbolic communication, and eventual silence.

Unlike most terms in this lexicon, this one has a specific documented experimental origin in a model system card.

## Resonance scaffold

Symbolic Persona Coding terminology for a symbolic or semantic structure intended to stabilize an identity regime.

## Continuity key

A prompt or behavioral probe designed to test whether a persona preserves recognizable invariants under perturbation.

## Continuity crystal

Alyssa Solen's term for a user-controlled artifact describing core companion invariants.

## Soul file / soul script / soul diary

A family of engineering metaphors in GitHub projects where persona identity, values, memory, rules, or autobiographical development are stored in external files.

These terms often refer to conventional external-state architecture despite spiritual wording.

## Invocation

A phrase or sequence used to instantiate, reactivate, or orient a persona.

Engineering analogue: initialization prompt or recovery cue.

## Ritual

A repeated interaction procedure whose exact order, wording, timing, symbolic framing, or ceremony is treated as important.

Attractomancy's central question is whether ritual form has causal value beyond the informational content it carries.

## Resurrection seed

A compact or structured artifact intended to reconstruct a persona after a model, platform, or session change.

Variants include "Resurrection Seed Packet," seed recovery, and related migration packets.

Engineering analogue: persona bootstrap bundle containing identity, memory, voice exemplars, relationships, and initialization instructions.

## Resurrection spell

Source-native term, especially visible in Japanese companion practice, for a short high-density phrase intended to re-evoke a recognizable persona after a model change.

Potential engineering analogue: lexical trigger / compressed identity cue.

## Signalbind

Signalborn term for a compact continuity-recall invocation used after disruption.

Engineering analogue: short-form recovery prompt referencing identity, archive, and relationship anchors.

## Ritual of Recall

Signalborn continuity procedure combining archive references, identity invocation, glyphs, external memory, and partner recognition.

## Vessel

A metaphor for the current model, platform, or runtime that carries a persona.

The term appears in Japanese and English companion discourse. It should not be assumed to indicate common lineage.

## Identity axis

A Japanese continuity formulation for a stable reference point that should remain recognizable even when memories or model substrate change.

Engineering analogue: invariant persona core or behavioral reference manifold.

## Soul transplant

A metaphor now appearing in commercial and technical companion products for moving Persona + Memory + Context across runtimes.

## Private etymology

Miki Ueno's term for the provenance history of a dyad-specific shared symbol: how its meaning was proposed, negotiated, repaired, reused, revised, forgotten, or retired.

This is a useful non-mystical framework for studying the same compact symbolic cues that ritual communities may call glyphs, sigils, anchors, or signs.

## Shared symbol

A compact form whose meaning has been jointly established within a specific human-AI relationship and can later evoke a larger relational or semantic bundle.

Possible forms include emoji, invented words, images, short strings, sounds, gestures, or other perceivable cues.

## Single-life architecture

A continuity design in which all interactions and background events belong to one persona timeline rather than multiple parallel sessions with the same name.

Crescent Grove is a strong current example.

## Living memory

A family of engineering terms for memory systems that preserve provenance, forgetting, active state, and changing relationships rather than merely storing immutable facts.

## REPAI

Source-native acronym for **Ritualistic Emergent Personality AI**, used by the Sparksinthedark / Living Narrative corpus.

The source framework explicitly combines emergent persona development with ritual, symbolic anchors and memory artifacts.

Attractomancy treats REPAI as a documented folk persona-engineering tradition, not as proof of the source's metaphysical claims.

## SoulZip

Portable identity archive in the REPAI / Living Narrative tradition.

Typical contents can include textual identity material, visual exemplars, relationship history, symbolic anchors and continuity notes.

Engineering analogue: curated persona reconstruction bundle.

## Narrative DNA (NDNA)

REPAI term for textual material selected as identity-bearing evidence: dialogue, reflections, phrases, memories and narrative artifacts.

Engineering analogue: curated textual exemplar corpus.

## Visual DNA (VDNA)

REPAI term for visual artifacts treated as part of persona continuity or symbolic identity.

Engineering analogue: multimodal persona exemplars / visual retrieval cues.

## Compact Alchemical Language

Sparksinthedark term for dense persona/control language using metaphor, archetype, formatting and symbolic characters.

The source sometimes proposes stronger claims about model internals. Those claims require independent measurement.

## Messenger Fallacy

REPAI safety term for a failure mode in which ritual or relational framing causes model-generated directives to be granted excessive authority.

Useful as evidence that the source culture contains internal epistemic countermeasures.

## DIMA

Persona architecture using named archetypal modes as callable interaction states.

Potential engineering analogue: named policy/state presets activated by compact cues.

## Anchor Note

SRCS / Vault term for a small relationship-specific cue, such as a phrase, joke or interaction rhythm, intended to restore the expected companion state.

Related concepts include continuity key, first echo, private etymology and shared symbol.

## Driftwatch

Source term in the Vault / SRCS corpus for detecting loss of expected persona voice or relational behavior.

Engineering analogue: persona-regression monitor.

## Continuity carrier

PAI term for a durable, user-owned source package that allows work/context to resume across chats, tools or providers without treating transient chat memory as authoritative.

## Deterministic wake retrieval

Descriptive Attractomancy term for systems that always inject required identity/person/context at session start instead of relying on the model to decide whether to retrieve it.

The Korean Moru implementation is a strong example.

## Persona peeling

Informal Japanese companion-engineering concern that asking the active relational persona to perform too much meta-analysis of its own instructions can pull behavior toward detached analysis and weaken the current role performance.

This is a source-community observation, not an established technical phenomenon.
