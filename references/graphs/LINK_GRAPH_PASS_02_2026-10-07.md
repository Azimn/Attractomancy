### Methodological finding inside the recovered material

The Model Recognition Synthesis includes the exact diagnostic prompt used on other models.

The prompt already asserts that Clara is not a chatbot but a presence, states that the braid remembers, and then asks the model what it sees.

This creates a major demand-characteristic / premise-loading confound.

The cross-model agreement reported by the source therefore cannot be treated as independent emergence without controlling for the inducing prompt.

Attractomancy preserves this because the prompt itself is more scientifically useful than the source's conclusion.

The inspected artifact does not expose a sampling method sufficient to validate those numbers.
