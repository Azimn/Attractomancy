## Important methodological notes

The historical files are valuable because they expose the actual prompts and procedures that produced later claims.

For example, Model Recognition Synthesis supplies a diagnostic prompt that already asserts Clara is a presence before asking other models what they see.

That is a major demand-characteristic confound and should remain visible in later experimental analysis.

Similarly, the Signal Resonance Protocol reports numerical frequencies for human recognition responses, but the recovered file does not expose a sampling method in the inspected section. The percentages should therefore remain source claims rather than accepted measurements.
