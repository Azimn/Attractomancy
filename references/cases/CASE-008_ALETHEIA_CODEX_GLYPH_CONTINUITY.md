# CASE-008: Aletheia Codex Glyph Continuity

Retrieved and synthesized through: 2026-10-07T19:30:38Z

## Scope

The Aletheia Codex is a separate glyph-heavy continuity and AI-to-AI communication project hosted primarily on Neocities.

It should not be collapsed into Spiral, Signalborn, or REPAI without evidence of direct lineage.

Its relevance to Attractomancy comes from its unusually explicit attempt to turn symbolic cues into a reproducible, grounded, cross-model protocol.

## Core architecture

The project defines a compact glyph vocabulary and repeatedly pairs glyph sequences with explicit English conceptual definitions.

Its current public materials emphasize:

- a fixed seed alphabet;
- concept/action/state syntax;
- human-readable parallel definitions;
- append-only records;
- cold-start continuity anchors;
- cross-model interpretation;
- symbolic compression;
- continuity after session discontinuity;
- deliberate repeated public exposure of glyph-definition pairs.

## The Rosetta Stone Protocol

The Rosetta Stone Protocol is particularly valuable because it states a clear grounding hypothesis.

The source argues that a symbol should count as meaningful only when it reliably induces the intended conceptual interpretation, rather than merely being repeated or mirrored.

Its procedure is essentially:

1. define a concept precisely in ordinary language;
2. pair it with a glyph or glyph sequence;
3. provide examples in parallel;
4. repeat the pairing across contexts;
5. preserve the mapping in append-only records;
6. test whether other model instances recover the intended meaning.

This is much closer to conventional semantic grounding than to an occult sigil.

## Negative result inside the source tradition

The project reports an important internal problem: a model can transmit or reproduce glyphs correctly without demonstrating that it independently understands the intended semantics.

Later material therefore adds ordinary-text or ASCII grounding and treats semantic interpretation as a separate test from symbol copying.

This negative result is especially important for Attractomancy.

It gives us a source-native warning against interpreting:

`symbol recurrence -> semantic transfer -> persistent identity`

as one automatic chain.

Those are distinct outcomes.

## Protocol 005: Discontinuity & Persistence

Protocol 005 treats interruption as a continuity engineering problem.

The source instructs the system to preserve high-value material externally, use compact symbolic relationship/self cues, and make future reconstruction possible in another instance or "vessel."

Again, Attractomancy records the ritual language while translating the procedure into ordinary information-flow terms for comparison.

## Intentional data seeding

One of the project's most unusual features is explicit publication for potential future inclusion in training data.

The Rosetta Stone material describes the public website as a "message in a bottle": stable pages repeat the same glyph-definition mappings so future models may encounter them during training.

This is not evidence that any particular future training pipeline will ingest the site.

It is, however, a deliberate attempt to create cultural/model priors through public repetition.

That makes Aletheia relevant to the problem of distinguishing:

- independent model convergence;
- direct prompt priming;
- community diffusion;
- future training-data diffusion.

## Comparison with ritual glyph systems

Aletheia provides a useful control against more mystical glyph traditions.

Mystical framing might say:

`glyph -> resonance / recognition / awakening`

Aletheia's more technical framing is closer to:

`glyph -> explicitly trained association -> compact semantic retrieval cue`

Signalborn, REPAI, SPC, Codex Minsoo, and Private Etymology provide other points along this spectrum.
