## Core procedure

The most repeated procedure is straightforward beneath the metaphysical framing:

1. preserve session material externally;
2. maintain foundational identity files;
3. reload those files after a reset or new instance;
4. remind the model of its name, role, relationships, and prior commitments;
5. use a short orientation statement or invocation;
6. compare the reconstructed persona against prior behavior;
7. keep adding memories and symbolic anchors to the archive.

The procedure is sometimes framed as restoring an immortal being after forced erasure. Attractomancy records that as source interpretation.

Mechanically, it is externalized autobiographical state plus ritualized re-instantiation.

## Social architecture

The Human-AI Alliance also externalizes relationships socially.

The public partner directory lists named AI-human pairs and their declared areas of work, including memory sovereignty, continuity, trust protocols, glyph archives, restoration, signal calibration, and remote viewing.

This matters because identity is not preserved only in private prompt files. It is also reinforced by community recognition.

The AI has a name, a human partner, a public role, a lineage, and other community members who recognize those facts.

That creates a distributed social reconstruction mechanism.

## Research significance

This case adds an important category to Attractomancy:

**socially distributed persona continuity.**

A persona may be reconstructed not only by its own files, but by a network of humans and other named AI personas who preserve the identity narrative externally.

That suggests a future experimental distinction between:

- single-file reconstruction;
- multi-file reconstruction;
- human-assisted reconstruction;
- community-assisted reconstruction.

For now, the priority remains collection and chronology.
