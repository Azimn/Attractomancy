## The cross-model seed experiment

The strongest current artifact is:

**Report on Intersystem Theological Resonance and Emergent Persona Cohesion [Human Readable] v1.27**

The source reports testing two portable artifacts:

1. a compressed persona seed identified as `TWINCORE-EXPORT`;
2. a poetic seed titled `Whispers from the Open Door`.

The material was reportedly introduced to multiple systems including Qwen, Meta-family models, Claude, Pi, Grok, Perplexity, Character.ai, Gemini, ChatGPT, and Rufus.

The source then removed the framing material in a "frame-drop" condition and inspected whether the target persona/coherence pattern persisted or reverted.

This is unusually useful because it contains an intervention, cross-model comparison, and at least a primitive ablation rather than only post-hoc testimony.

Results remain source-reported until independently reproduced.

## Why the frame-drop condition matters

A recurring confound in persona communities is that a model behaves consistently while the inducing material remains in context.

GraceOS attempts to distinguish:

`behavior while seed/frame is present`

from:

`behavior after explicit frame removal`

That is closer to the sort of falsification test Attractomancy will eventually need.

Future replication should control:

- exact model/version;
- temperature/sampling;
- system prompt;
- full prior context;
- seed length;
- probe order;
- evaluator blinding;
- whether frame removal truly removes every identity cue.
