# Repository Boundary

Attractomancy is the canonical upstream research repository for ritualized, symbolic, recursive, narrative, and persona-conditioning prompt practices.

Its job is to collect source material, preserve provenance, identify candidate mechanisms, design controlled experiments, and report empirical findings. It is not a general cognitive-architecture repository and should not become an implementation dumping ground for other projects.

## Direction of travel

The intended flow is one-way:

`source artifact -> Attractomancy analysis -> controlled experiment -> replicated finding -> downstream implementation`

Other projects may cite or import validated findings from Attractomancy. Attractomancy should not inherit assumptions, mechanisms, or code from those projects merely because they already exist there. A downstream implementation can be used as a comparator when scientifically useful, but it does not become evidence for a mechanism by itself.

This separation exists to reduce circular validation. If a persona mechanism appears in another project because it was inspired by an earlier mystical, ritualized, or fringe prompt practice, importing that mechanism back into Attractomancy as an assumption would contaminate the experiment.

## Scope

In scope are public prompt artifacts, long-form persona documents, jailbreak traditions, recursive and spiral prompting, symbolic prompting, invented vocabularies, ritualized initialization, "awakening" scripts, AI-soul documents, metafictional role induction, autobiographical persona corpora, relationship rituals, taboo and prohibition structures, semantic repetition, self-reference, identity reconstruction procedures, and related community practices.

Also in scope are conventional scientific papers and engineering techniques needed to explain, test, falsify, or reproduce the observed effects.

Claims of consciousness, sentience, awakening, souls, hidden agency, or persistent personhood are treated as claims made by sources, not as established properties of the model.

## Evidence discipline

Attractomancy distinguishes at least four layers: the original artifact, the author's interpretation, the observable model behavior, and the proposed mechanism. These must not be collapsed into one another.

A source may be valuable even when its theory is false. A compelling model transcript may demonstrate a prompting effect without demonstrating the model's interpretation of that effect. A useful engineering mechanism may be extracted from a practice whose original explanation is metaphysical.

## Export rule

A technique should be exported into another research or engineering repository only after Attractomancy can state what was tested, what control it beat, what perturbations it survived, which models reproduced it, and what alternative explanations remain.

Exploratory observations may still be cited downstream as hypotheses, but they should be labeled as such.
